# Credit-Card-Transaction-Analysis-
A comprehensive Python and Power BI analytics project that analyzes 1.29M+ credit card transactions to uncover geographic patterns, transaction behaviors, customer segments, and fraudulent activity.

# Data Source - Kaggle
The data used was derived from Credit_Card_Transaction.csv

# Feature Engineering - Python
I utilized Python (Google Colab) to extract and analyze key features from large datasets, focusing on geospatial patterns, customer behavioral trends, and fraud detection.

As part of the analysis, I conducted geospatial distance modeling by leveraging customer latitude and longitude coordinates to identify location-based transaction patterns, anomalies, and potential fraudulent activities.

Additionally, I performed business category classification and fraud analytics, evaluating fraud trends across different business segments. This analysis incorporated factors such as business category, transaction value, transaction location (state), and time of transaction to uncover high-risk patterns, assess transaction behavior, and identify potential fraud indicators.

The insights derived from these analyses supported data-driven decision-making, enhanced fraud monitoring capabilities, and improved the understanding of customer and business transaction behavior. 

# Executive Dashbboard - PowerBI
The dashboard reveals strong revenue concentration among middle-aged customers, female customers, and weekend spending behavior, generating a total transaction value of 65.89 million.

# Findings
1. Customers aged 25-34 are the highest revenue contributors

The 25-34 age group generated the largest transaction amount at approximately 16.4M, making it the most valuable customer segment.
2. Female customers drive a larger share of revenue

Female customers generated 35.94M (54.54%) of total revenue.Male customers contributed 29.95M (45.46%).
3. Weekend and early-week spending outperform midweek transactions

Monday (12.88M) recorded the highest transaction value, narrowly surpassing Sunday (12.87M).
Saturday (10.91M) ranks third, highlighting strong weekend consumer activity
4. Grocery spending dominates customer expenditure

Grocery POS transactions (10.42M) represent the largest spending category by a significant margin
5. Geographic concentration is heavily skewed toward North America

Customer distribution shows the highest density of transactions across the United States, with additional activity across Europe, Africa, Asia, and Australia.
6. Fraud levels appear relatively low compared to legitimate transactions

Non-fraudulent transactions account for approximately 63.08M of the total revenue.
Fraudulent transactions total about 2.80M
# Business Implications
1. Focus marketing and retention efforts on the 25-44 age segments, which collectively generate the largest share of revenue.
2. Design campaigns and loyalty programs specifically targeting female customers, who currently contribute the majority of sales.
3. Schedule promotions around weekends and Mondays to capitalize on peak spending periods.
4. Strengthen partnerships and offers within grocery, shopping, and transportation categories, where customer spending is strongest.
5. Continue investing in fraud detection and prevention to further reduce the approximately 2.8M exposed to fraudulent activity.
